# Two-person feedback — supporting account

Source: the author's private playtest notes from 2026-11-08 (not published in this repository), read alongside PROCESS.md and [the materials audit](final-materials-audit.json). This is a condensed account of the author-supplied feedback recorded on 2026-11-08, not a new test conducted while preparing the merged PROCESS.

Two participants used Macs, with Safari, Firefox, Chrome and DuckDuckGo reported across their testing. They used independent identities, left sounds, answered one another, saw shared changes and chatted. Sounds and replies remained after refresh or reopening and when checked the next day.

Initially, using sounds to build the environment together was difficult to understand. Guiding words and default sounds were added in response. Changes in residents, creatures, objects and behaviour encouraged exploration and informed the emphasis on sounds with visible consequences.

The synthetic frog sound was disliked. After replacement, the reported listening judgement was acceptable. This is feedback about that change, not approval of every possible mix.

The account does not identify a specific test-session build, report measured completion times or establish a controlled browser comparison. Its provenance and the separate read-only delivery checks are recorded in [the original materials audit](final-materials-audit.json).
