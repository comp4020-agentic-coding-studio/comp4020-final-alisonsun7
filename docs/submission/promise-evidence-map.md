## Evidence categories and open gates

- **Automatic checks:** named source/build, database isolation, exact assertion,
  result and negative control where appropriate.
- **Developer-operated browser/visual review:** actual path and unmodified
  captures under stated browser, GPU, viewport and network. No generated
  concept picture is completion evidence.
- **Human listening:** named sound combination, listener/output/settings and
  specific judgement. Currently not recorded as completed.
- **Unfamiliar pairs:** [the two-group protocol](human-playtest.md) and real
  observations, assistance and consequent change. Currently NOT RUN.
- **Return/deployment:** a real next-day session and the exact authorized Fly
  release are distinct from today's reload/restart/local promotion. Neither
  is claimed complete by this map.

asks the product argument, agent rules, checks and app to agree. The map makes
that agreement inspectable; it does not award a grade or replace the author's
judgement.
