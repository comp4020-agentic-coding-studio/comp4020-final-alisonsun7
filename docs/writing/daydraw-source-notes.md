# Sources for the product argument

Preparation checked these original sources. That does not mean Wen read or endorses them. Read before adopting the candidate README. Sources for research-note require a separate field-level literature argument.

## Robin Sloan, An app can be a home-cooked meal

https://www.robinsloan.com/notes/home-cooked-app/

A family app illustrates value grounded in a small known group. DayDraw's adaptation: usefulness to an outing instead of growth. Difference: new invitees still need discoverable onboarding. This source does not prove card allocation fairness, live voting usability or low social pressure.

## Aurora Harley, Visibility of System Status

https://www.nngroup.com/articles/visibility-system-status/

Status and action feedback help someone understand current state. DayDraw's adaptation: pending/confirmed/error, phase labels and reconnect state. Difference: making vote totals visible could also pressure decisions. Human evidence is needed to assess that cost.
