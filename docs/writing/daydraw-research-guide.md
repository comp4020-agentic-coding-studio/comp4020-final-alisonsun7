# COMP8020 research-note preparation

This is a research writing framework, not a ready-to-submit essay. Final output goes in root research-note.md. Existing reading/notes are authoritative; inspect before adding. C8 does not require the note, but start reading by the C9 stage.

## Candidate position — Wen must choose or revise

“Good agentic development depends on turning product values into behavioural checks and reviewing what those checks cannot establish.” This reaches beyond DayDraw if argued with literature about verification, human judgement and limits of agent evaluation. DayDraw can illustrate a point; its own test counts are not scholarly evidence about the field.

## An argument to investigate

- Thesis: articulate one precise claim about agentic practice.
- Support: weigh work on autonomous software agents and behaviour evaluation against practitioner accounts of harnesses and observability.
- Counter-case: fast generation and passing benchmarks can still have value; requiring exhaustive checks has costs and may freeze exploration.
- Response: justify a scoped combination of automated backpressure and human product judgement; specify where the claim applies.
- Consequence: what should practitioners do differently and what remains uncertain?

## Reading workflow

Wen lists the scholarly and practitioner texts actually encountered across the course. Claude can locate primary sources, verify titles/authors/year/links, and help extract the claim each makes. Read the actual source before quoting or asserting results. No fabricated bibliography or unseen paper summaries. If a source cannot be accessed, mark the limitation and do not pretend to evaluate it.

A literature matrix can use: source; central claim; evidence/method; limit; relationship to my thesis; strongest counterpoint. Evaluate contrasts, not a sequence of summaries. Use 600–800 words as final guidance after actual reading. Confirm expected citation format from current course/template.

## Writing separation

README: why this app's choices count as good.
PROCESS: how this app was actually directed, built and checked.
Research note: what constitutes good practice in the field, argued from literature.

Do not submit this guide as research-note.md. Ask for Wen's actual thesis/notes, help organise and critique them, and preserve their voice. No invented scholarly findings, quotes or personal reading history.
