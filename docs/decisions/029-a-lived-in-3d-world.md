# ADR 029 — A lived-in 3D world with ordinary private rooms

Date: 2026-11-08. Status: locally verified and playable as application `37e055b`.

## Request and priority

After trying the two flat presentations, Wen chooses the existing 3D planet.
The reported problems are strong jagged edges, a small and underpopulated café,
unconvincing private-room interiors, an odd invitation to test a resident's
toilet lock, and flickering conservatory surfaces while walking.

3D remains the main presentation. ADR028's optional view studies and original
coastal history remain available but are not receiving parallel art expansion.
This is focused refinement, not a new engine, room or life-simulation system.
The core remains friends answering real sounds to make a lasting shared place.

## Decisions and costs

- Keep the existing MSAA renderer. It already used antialiasing; the old 1.5×
  DPR ceiling was not an absence of AA. Use a bounded 1.25–2× drawing scale,
  recomputed on resize, capped at six million pixels. This improves sampling of
  thin silhouettes and uses retina resolution without unbounded 4K workload.
  It does not remove the authored low-poly style or promise perfectly smooth
  shadows on every device. No post-processing dependency is added.
- Make the café shell 1.5× wider and 1.35× deeper at the exact existing centre.
  Furnishings keep their human-scale dimensions; six tables and a wider counter
  use an actual raised terrace. Walking height, inside lighting and wall
  collision match the enlarged footprint. Historical sounds stay where their
  authors left them. Six café neighbours have stable identities, three cat and
  three dog silhouettes, different clothes/facing and subtle idle movement.
  They remain NPCs; real sound responses override decorative idle gestures.
- Remove both visible resident-test entrances and their client handlers from
  planet/coast. NPCs still visit naturally, through the same authoritative gate
  as players. Keep the scheduling endpoint for deterministic scratch fixtures
  and historical API compatibility; never present it as a social activity.
- Distinguish private rooms through real 3D fixtures: a toilet with seat,
  cistern and water, sink and mirror; a shower tray, head, taps, drain, toiletries,
  towel and changing bench. They leave the central entry/exit clear and retain
  existing private-stay audio and occupancy admission. No false shared sound
  rows or permanent player history are generated by furnishings.
- Replace the conservatory's full planter lids with four real rim pieces and
  lower soil. The former soil and cream lid both ended at y=.52, producing
  actual depth fighting. Geometric separation solves that conflict directly.
  Also remove the duplicate coplanar central Green patch. Raising arbitrary
  material bias would disguise these overlapping surfaces rather than fix them.

## Verification boundary

Use fresh scratch databases and ports for writing specifications and browser
flows. Check actual 3D screenshots at both marking sizes, walk the café and
conservatory, exercise player restroom/shower visits and second-person lock
rejection, preserve quiet preference and source response. A unit test on the
frozen preceding geometry must fail for the missing fixtures/planter separation
and old café boundary. Verify the real candidate, not just isolated meshes.

The HD assessment remains separate: `docs/final-quality-review-2026-11-08.md`
identifies human listening, unfamiliar-pair evidence, author-reviewed arguments
and the course deployment as gaps. More NPCs, more checks or AA alone do not
establish that the shared sound experience is worth returning to.

Local promotion must retain all current rows. Code rollback uses the preceding
compiled application with the current database, never a restored old DB. No
public deployment, main push, permission change or schema migration is included.

## Actual outcome and correction

The first scene run passed its mechanical checks but screenshot inspection
found a real visual failure: the old low café name board filled the camera
after walking inside. It was moved to the front gable and included in camera
avoidance. The same walked position now shows people, tables, cups and counter.
This failure is retained rather than represented as a clean first attempt.
Keyboard V now synchronises the header on the next rendered frame without
rewriting unchanged DOM each frame.

Final-source checks: 245 specifications across 37 files, then 21 actual scene
browser checks and inspected captures. Earlier checks on the furnished candidate
passed 31 private-room and 24 shared-neighbour behaviours; these precede only
the sign/camera/header corrections. Raw records distinguish these build scopes.
The final scene path also walks both furnished private rooms. It compares the
same soil angles before/after a strafe; the preceding coplanar triangles are
visible in the frozen baseline and absent in those candidate captures. That is
a bounded observation, not a universal no-flicker claim.

The local promotion preserved every row in all twelve current tables, including
29 source rows, and verified 52 served module/style byte hashes. An unjoined
observer subsequently loaded the actual promoted 3D page, found the nine prior
public sources, six explicitly marked café NPCs and both fixture sets, with no
write request or page error. See `docs/evidence/lived-in-3d-2026-11-08.json`.
