# 022 — A recognisable place made of shared sounds

2026-11-07. Wen clarified the priority: coherent, harmonious and distinctive
style matters more than cinematic beauty. The product's distinguishing
experience is making a sound environment together and enjoying interaction
with other people. D is an atmosphere reference, not the acceptance target
for future photorealism work. This supersedes the visual-fidelity priority
in ADRs 020–021 while retaining their useful scene assets and rollback tags.

## One visual language, one primary activity

Keep warm paper, timber, sea green and the original animal people. Blue means
water/wind, green means animals and orange means people, consistently across
the home-page rings, shared contribution cards and actual three-dimensional
sound marks. Colour has text labels. No new sound or background bed is added.

The first invitation is to listen, leave a sound for someone else and answer
what they find. Walking instructions remain available as secondary help.
The landscape gives people somewhere to do this; scene and weather controls
come after the shared sound experience in the existing native drawer.

## Make the existing cooperation discoverable

- A compact **Our soundscape** entry shows real joined people and active public
  sounds. The count includes the current joined person, excludes NPCs and
  unjoined spectators, and does not count historical authors as online people.
  It opens the same native drawer as Tools, preserving one focus scope.
- The drawer names authors, places and existing reply relationships. **Face
  sound** turns without moving or placing. **Conversation** opens the existing
  public relation view. **Reply with a sound** is offered beside a contribution
  within the existing reach; the existing cancellable reply flow remains.
- **While you’re here** keeps at most four public changes received in this
  visit, deduplicated across SSE and acknowledgements. The active contribution
  list is not a complete history. A reconnect snapshot never invents missed
  activity. Stay sounds from toilets/showers are excluded from the public list,
  counts, activity and reply threads.
- A low, lightweight 3D thread connects nearby live public replies. Endpoints
  follow the actual floor, while audio keeps its original height and rules.
  Removing a parent hides its thread without removing or reviving the child.
  A moving dot means the child recording is actually playing; silence, hidden
  pages and reduced motion do not imply playback.

There are no new scores, daily tasks, ownership rules, recording features,
room splits or automatic placements. Existing room limits, public removal,
idempotent operations, authoritative hut admission and persistence stay intact.
No server protocol or database schema change is required.
