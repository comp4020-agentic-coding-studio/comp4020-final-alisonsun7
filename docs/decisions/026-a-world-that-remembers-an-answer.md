The 2026-11-07 brief replaces feature expansion with one authored claim:
friends without musical training can recognise a place changed because one
answered the other. The current run at source7d40be6 was played before edits
on a cloned database at18884: private Bird sounds preview, placement, direct

small characters, simultaneous labels and a list/line result obscured their

Keep Three.js, the sphere and the server's identity/durable reply system.
Use a close activity camera and an explicit overview, rather than framing the
globe by default. A new2D engine could improve control but would consume the
work needed for sound and expressive results, and risk existing coordinates
and collision. Cinematic assets and nine separate games are rejected. The
three authored carriers are a canopy at Listening Green, a beat flower at
Pocket Jam Stage and small lights at Reading Nook; all other places remain
explorable. Original object geometry, a cohesive paper/wood palette and a
single contextual action take priority over decorative labels.

Works are server projections of existing public contribution/reply history,
including removed endpoints. Root source ID gives stable identity. Live
water+birds form a habitat, melody+rhythm an ensemble, other combinations a
lantern. Any pairing gets feedback; a living cross-author reply opens the
shared canopy/halo. The distinct participant IDs originate from HttpOnly
cookie identities; two same-cookie tabs, NPCs and a local kangaroo never
qualify as two contributors. Removed sounds lose their live effect but keep
authorship and relationship history. No schema change or coordinate rewrite
is needed. Replay and late join derive from the same source graph.
